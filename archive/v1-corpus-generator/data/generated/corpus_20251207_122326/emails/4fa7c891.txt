I hope this message finds you well. I'm writing to remind everyone about our parking lot procedures and safety guidelines as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Arrive early to allow time for parking and to avoid congestion during student drop-off
• Do not block fire lanes or emergency access routes
• Drive slowly through the parking lot, especially during arrival and dismissal times
• Be mindful of students and families in the lot

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our school safe and organized.
