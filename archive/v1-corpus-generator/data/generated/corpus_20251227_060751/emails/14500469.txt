Dear Angela, Brittany, and Sarah,

I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Please do not block loading zones or fire lanes
• Arrive early to allow time for parking during peak hours
• Drive slowly through the lot and watch for students and pedestrians
• Ensure your vehicle is locked when unattended

The parking lot can get quite congested during arrival and dismissal times, so your patience and cooperation help keep our school community safe.

If you have any questions about parking assignments or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to the main office.

Thank you for your attention to these important safety measures.
