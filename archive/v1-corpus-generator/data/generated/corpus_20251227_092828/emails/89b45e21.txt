Hi Angela and Randy,

I wanted to reach out regarding some adjustments we need to implement across our support services framework, effective next month.

As we discussed in our meeting last week, we've identified several areas where our current approach to serving students with diverse learning needs requires revision. The changes will affect how we structure our one-on-one support sessions, classroom placement strategies, and the documentation process for individualized learning plans.

Specifically:

• We're revising how students transition between resource settings and general education environments. This will require updated protocols for monitoring progress in both contexts.

• The paperwork associated with personalized learning plans will now require quarterly reviews instead of annual ones. This means the forms we currently use will need to be redesigned.

• We're implementing a new framework for how we document the supports and adjustments each student receives. The old system will be phased out by the end of the quarter.

• Staff training on the revised placement procedures will be mandatory for anyone involved in determining appropriate settings for our learners.

I know this represents a significant shift from what we discussed at the beginning of the year. I'd like to schedule time with both of you this week to walk through the implementation timeline and address any concerns.
