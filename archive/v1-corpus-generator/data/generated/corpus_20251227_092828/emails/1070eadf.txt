Dear Kristin, Dawn, and Anne,

I hope this message finds you well. I'm writing to inform you of some important changes coming to how we structure support for our learners who require enhanced assistance.

As we discussed in our meeting last month, the administrative team has decided to revise our current framework for serving students with documented learning differences. These adjustments will take effect beginning next semester.

Key points regarding what we'll be implementing:

• The documentation process for eligible students will now follow a streamlined protocol that we'll review together
• Service delivery models will shift to better reflect what works best for each individual learner
• The way we coordinate between general classroom settings and targeted support will be restructured
• Family communication procedures will be updated to ensure clearer dialogue about each student's progress and needs

These changes represent our commitment to providing more personalized pathways for students who learn differently. We'll be holding training sessions next month to walk everyone through the new procedures and answer questions.

Please review the attached documentation at your earliest convenience. I'd like to schedule a follow-up meeting with all of you by end of week to discuss implementation details and address any concerns.

Thank you for your partnership in this important work.
