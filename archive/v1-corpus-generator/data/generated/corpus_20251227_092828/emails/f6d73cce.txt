Dear Wendy and Ryan,

I wanted to reach out regarding the adjustments we've been planning for our support services framework, which we touched on during our meeting last month.

As discussed, we're moving forward with revisions to how we structure support for students who require additional assistance in their learning environments. The changes will affect several key areas:

1. Documentation and Planning Processes: We're streamlining the paperwork and planning procedures that families and staff currently use. This will reduce administrative burden while maintaining the quality of individualized support.

2. Service Delivery Models: We're expanding how students access support within their regular classroom settings and through specialized resource periods. The goal is to ensure each learner receives what they need in the most effective manner.

3. Staff Training and Resources: Professional development sessions will be scheduled for all building administrators and classroom teachers to ensure consistency across the district.

4. Family Communication: We'll be updating our notification procedures and meeting formats to make them more accessible and transparent.

I'd like to schedule a working session with both of you this week to discuss implementation timelines and any concerns you might have. Please let me know your availability.

These changes represent our commitment to providing equitable support for all learners in our care.
