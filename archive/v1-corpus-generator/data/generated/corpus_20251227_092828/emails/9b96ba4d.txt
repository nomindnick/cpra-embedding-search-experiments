Hi Kristin,

I wanted to reach out regarding the adjustments we've been planning for our support services framework. As discussed in our meeting last month, we need to communicate some important changes to our approach for students requiring additional support.

Effective next quarter, we'll be implementing a revised structure for how we deliver targeted assistance to learners with varying needs. This will impact several aspects of our current practices:

- The documentation process for individualized support plans will be streamlined to reduce administrative burden
- We're adjusting how we integrate students across different classroom settings and peer groups
- The tools and resources available to classroom staff will be expanded
- Our approach to how students access core curriculum will be reconsidered

I'd like you to prepare communications for building principals, teachers, and families explaining these shifts. The messaging should emphasize that we remain committed to ensuring all students have access to appropriate support, while also clarifying how our methods will evolve.

Please draft talking points that address:
- What these changes mean for student services
- How staff roles may shift
- Timeline for implementation
- How families will be involved in the process

Can you have a preliminary version ready by end of week? I'd like to review it before we roll this out to stakeholders.
