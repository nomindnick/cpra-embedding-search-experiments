Hi Christina, Anne, and Mark,

I wanted to reach out regarding the changes we've been planning for our learners who require enhanced instructional support. Following our discussions from last month, we're moving forward with restructuring how we deliver services to this population.

Key points for your awareness:

• We're revising how students with documented learning differences will receive their tailored instruction and support services. The framework we discussed—regarding how these individuals access curriculum—will be implemented starting next quarter.

• For those students currently working under formal service agreements (both the 13-category framework and the civil rights protection pathway), we'll be updating how their individualized plans are documented and communicated to families.

• We're also adjusting staffing and resource allocation in the learning support wing. This affects how our resource personnel will be deployed across buildings.

• Training sessions on the new service delivery model will be scheduled for all relevant staff members. I'll send calendar invites once we finalize dates.

The goal is to ensure that students working below grade-level benchmarks continue receiving appropriate levels of support while improving coordination across departments. What we discussed regarding the situation at the elementary school particularly influenced these decisions.

Please let me know if you have questions about how this impacts your areas. I'm available to discuss further details at your convenience.
