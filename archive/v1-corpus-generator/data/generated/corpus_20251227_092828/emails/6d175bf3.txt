I wanted to reach out regarding the changes we've been planning for our student support services framework. As discussed in our meeting last month, we're moving forward with restructuring how we deliver targeted assistance to learners who require additional support.

The key shifts include:

• Enhanced collaboration between general classroom teachers and our support staff to better integrate services within the regular learning environment

• Updated documentation procedures for students receiving individualized support plans—we'll be streamlining the paperwork that families receive

• Revised service delivery models that aim to reduce the separation of certain students from their peer groups during the school day

• New protocols for adjusting curriculum and classroom materials to better match individual student needs

I know this represents a significant change from our current approach, and we'll need clear communication with all stakeholders. Can we schedule time next week to discuss the rollout timeline and how we'll inform families about what's changing?
