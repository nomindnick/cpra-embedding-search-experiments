Hi Mark, Kristin, and Luis,

I wanted to reach out regarding the changes we discussed at last week's meeting about our support services structure for students who require additional assistance.

As you know, we've been evaluating how we deliver services to learners with varying abilities and needs. The administrative team has approved moving forward with several adjustments to our current approach:

1. We will be restructuring how we document support plans for eligible students. The forms and processes we currently use will be updated by the end of the month.

2. There will be changes to how we place students across different classroom settings. We're working toward a more flexible approach to where and how these learners receive instruction.

3. Support services personnel will be receiving updated guidelines on what types of in-class and pullout support we can provide. This affects how we structure the day for these students.

4. We need to revise our communication protocol with families regarding their child's progress and the services being provided.

I'll be scheduling individual meetings with each of you to discuss how these changes impact your specific roles. Please let me know your availability for next week.

In the meantime, if you have questions about what we discussed previously or need clarification on any of this, please reach out.
