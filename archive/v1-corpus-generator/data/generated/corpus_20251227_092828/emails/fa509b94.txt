I wanted to reach out regarding the changes we've been planning for our support services structure, which we touched on during our meeting last month.

As discussed, we're moving forward with revisions to how we deliver targeted interventions for learners who require additional support. The framework we developed will affect several aspects of our current service delivery model.

Key points to note:

• The documentation process for individual student support plans will be streamlined. We'll be updating the forms and procedures that outline what each student needs to succeed.

• Classroom teachers will have expanded involvement in the planning and implementation of support strategies. This represents a shift from what we've been doing.

• We're adjusting how we structure time and resources for students working with our support team. The logistics of this will roll out over the next quarter.

• Professional development sessions will be scheduled to ensure everyone understands the new framework and how it impacts their role.
