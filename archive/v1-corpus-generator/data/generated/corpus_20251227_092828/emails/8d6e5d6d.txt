I hope this message finds you well. Following our discussions from last quarter regarding the resources made available through the comprehensive support framework we reviewed in early 2021, I wanted to touch base on how we're moving forward with the implementation.

As you'll recall, we received notification about supplemental resources designated for educational institutions during the challenging period. We've been working through the administrative requirements to ensure proper stewardship of these funds, and I wanted to update you on our approach.

Our team has developed a strategic framework for how these resources will be deployed across the district. The allocation takes into account:

• Technology infrastructure improvements that were deferred during the situation at our facilities
• Learning support enhancements for students who experienced disruptions
• Professional development initiatives for staff adaptation
• Facility upgrades that support our operational resilience

We're targeting a phased approach that extends through the next fiscal cycle, ensuring we maximize impact while maintaining compliance with the oversight requirements that accompany this matter.
