Dear Carla, Natalie, and Craig,

I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy morning and afternoon dismissal times.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Arrive early enough to allow adequate time for parking before the school day begins
• Keep walkways and fire lanes clear at all times
• Drive slowly through the lot and watch for students and pedestrians
• Do not block other vehicles or loading zones

With the increase in traffic during peak hours, it's important that we all work together to keep our parking areas safe and organized. If you have any questions about parking assignments or need assistance locating your designated spot, please don't hesitate to reach out to the main office.

Thank you for your cooperation and attention to these important safety guidelines.
