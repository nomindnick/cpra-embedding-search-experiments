I wanted to send out a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures to ensure smooth traffic flow during arrival and dismissal times.

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Do not block fire lanes or loading zones
• Arrive early enough to avoid congestion during student drop-off and pick-up times
