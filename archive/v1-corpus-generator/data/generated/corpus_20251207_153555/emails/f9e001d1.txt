Dear Colleagues,

I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures to ensure smooth traffic flow and safety for all staff and students.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Staff parking is designated in Lot B. Please do not park in student or visitor spaces.
• Arrive early enough to allow time for parking and getting to your classroom before the bell rings.
• Keep your vehicle locked and do not leave valuables visible in your car.
• Be mindful of traffic patterns during arrival and dismissal times—please use designated entrance and exit routes.
• If you need accessible parking, please contact the main office to arrange a designated spot.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining an orderly and safe parking environment. If you have any questions or concerns about parking, please don't hesitate to reach out.
