I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy morning and afternoon dismissal times.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Arrive early enough to avoid congestion during student drop-off and pick-up times
• Keep the loading zones clear for buses and parent vehicles
• Drive slowly through the lot and watch for students
• Do not block fire lanes or emergency access areas

I know mornings can be hectic, but following these guidelines helps keep our students safe and keeps traffic flowing smoothly. If you have any questions about parking assignments or need clarification on any of these procedures, please let me know.
