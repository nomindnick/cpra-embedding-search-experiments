I wanted to send out a quick reminder about parking procedures at our school facilities. As we head into the busy season, please keep the following guidelines in mind:

• Please use designated staff parking areas only
• Reserved spots for administrators and visitors should remain clear
• Do not park in fire lanes or emergency access areas
• Visitor parking is available in the front lot near the main entrance

Thank you for your cooperation and attention to these important safety measures.
