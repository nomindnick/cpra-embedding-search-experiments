I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Do not block loading zones or fire lanes
• Arrive early to allow time for parking, especially on mornings with early events
• Report any parking issues or blocked spaces to the main office
• Visitor parking is available in the front lot for parents and guests

These guidelines help ensure safety and keep traffic flowing smoothly during arrival and dismissal times. If you have any questions or concerns about parking assignments, please reach out to the main office.
