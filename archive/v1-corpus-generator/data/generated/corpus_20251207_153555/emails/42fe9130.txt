I wanted to send a quick reminder about parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season. Please help reinforce these guidelines with your teams:

• Arrive early to allow adequate time for parking
• Use designated staff parking areas only
• Drive slowly through parking lots (5 mph speed limit)
• Be mindful of student drop-off and pick-up times
