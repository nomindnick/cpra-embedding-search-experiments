I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures to ensure smooth traffic flow and safety for everyone on campus.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Arrive early to allow adequate time for parking during peak hours
• Keep the loading zones clear for student drop-off and pick-up

If you have any questions about parking assignments or need a new parking pass, please stop by the main office.
