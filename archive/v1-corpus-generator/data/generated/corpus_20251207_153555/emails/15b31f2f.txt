I hope this message finds you well. I'm writing to share some important updates regarding parking procedures at our district facilities for the upcoming school year.

Effective immediately, we ask that all staff members:

• Display valid parking permits on vehicle dashboards at all times
• Park only in designated staff parking areas
• Avoid parking in visitor or student designated spaces
• Report any unauthorized vehicles to building administration

Additionally, we will be implementing new traffic flow patterns during morning arrival (7:30-8:15 AM) and afternoon dismissal (2:45-3:30 PM) to improve safety and reduce congestion. Please review the attached diagram for updated procedures.

If you have any questions or concerns about these parking guidelines, please don't hesitate to reach out to your building principal or contact me directly.

Thank you for your attention to these important safety measures.
