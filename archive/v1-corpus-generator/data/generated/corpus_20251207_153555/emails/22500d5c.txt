I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Staff parking is designated in Lot B only. Please do not park in student or visitor spaces.
• Display your staff parking pass on your dashboard at all times.
• The loading zone near the main entrance is for drop-off/pick-up only—please do not leave vehicles unattended.
• Arrive early enough to find parking before the morning bell rings to avoid congestion.
• If you need to leave during the school day, please use the designated exit route to minimize disruption.

If you have any questions about parking assignments or need a replacement parking pass, please reach out to the main office.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our parking areas safe and organized!
