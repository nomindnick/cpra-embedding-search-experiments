I wanted to reach out with a friendly reminder about our staff parking procedures. With the start of the new semester, we've had some congestion in the parking lot during arrival and dismissal times.

A few reminders:
- Please use designated staff parking areas only
- Arrive early enough to park before the student drop-off window (7:45-8:15 AM)

These simple steps help keep traffic flowing smoothly and ensure student safety. Thanks for your cooperation!
