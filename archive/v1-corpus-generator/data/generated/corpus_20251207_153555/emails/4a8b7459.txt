Good afternoon Michael, Brent, and Maria,

I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces in the main lot
• Do not block fire lanes or loading zones at any time
• Arrive early enough to secure a spot before student drop-off begins
• Remember that visitor parking is reserved for parents and guests
• If you need an accessible space, please contact the main office

I've noticed a few vehicles parked in student areas during the day, which creates congestion during dismissal. Let's all do our part to keep the lot organized and safe.

If you have any questions or concerns about parking, please reach out to the main office.

Thank you for your cooperation!
