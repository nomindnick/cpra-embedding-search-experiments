Good morning Mark, Maria, and Joshua,

I wanted to send out a quick reminder about our staff parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Do not block loading zones or fire lanes
• Arrive early enough to secure a spot before student drop-off begins
• Display your parking permit on your dashboard at all times
• Report any parking violations or safety concerns to the main office

We've had a few incidents recently with congestion during morning arrival and afternoon dismissal. Your cooperation in following these guidelines helps keep our parking areas safe and organized for everyone.

If you have any questions about parking assignments or need a replacement permit, please contact the main office.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.
