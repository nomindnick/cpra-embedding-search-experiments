I wanted to send out a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Keep the loading zones clear during arrival and dismissal times
• Do not park in visitor or student designated areas
• Ensure your vehicle is locked at all times
• Report any parking violations or safety concerns to the main office

Our parking lot is shared space, and adhering to these guidelines helps keep traffic flowing smoothly and ensures student safety. If you have any questions about parking assignments or need a parking permit, please contact the main office.
