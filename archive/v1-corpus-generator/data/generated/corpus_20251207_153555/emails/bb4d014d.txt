Dear Dennis and Joyce,

I hope this message finds you well. I'm writing to remind everyone about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following guidelines in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces in the lot
• Arrive early enough to avoid congestion during student drop-off times
• Do not block fire lanes or emergency access routes
• Keep your vehicle locked and unattended valuables secured
• Report any parking violations or safety concerns to the main office

With increased traffic during morning arrival and afternoon dismissal, I encourage everyone to be patient and courteous. If you have any questions about parking assignments or need a different spot due to accessibility needs, please contact the main office.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our parking areas safe and organized.
