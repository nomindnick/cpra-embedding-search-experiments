Hi Kimberly and Brittany,

I wanted to send out a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please remember to:
- Park only in designated staff parking areas
- Keep your vehicle parked during the school day
- Avoid blocking any fire lanes or emergency access routes
- Be mindful of student drop-off and pick-up times, especially between 7:45-8:15 AM and 2:45-3:15 PM

If you need to move your vehicle during the day, please notify the main office first. Also, if you notice any parking violations or safety concerns in the lot, please report them to administration.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our parking areas safe and organized!
