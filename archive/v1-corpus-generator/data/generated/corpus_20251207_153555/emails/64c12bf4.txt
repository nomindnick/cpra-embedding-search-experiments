Dear Laura, Martin, and Daniel,

I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces in the faculty lot
• Allow extra time in the morning for parking and arrival
• Do not block fire lanes or loading zones at any time
• Ensure your vehicle is properly registered with the main office
• Be mindful of student drop-off and pick-up times (7:45-8:15 AM and 2:45-3:15 PM)

The parking lot can get congested during peak hours, so arriving a few minutes earlier can help ease congestion for everyone.

If you have any questions about parking assignments or need to register a new vehicle, please contact the main office.

Thank you for your cooperation!
