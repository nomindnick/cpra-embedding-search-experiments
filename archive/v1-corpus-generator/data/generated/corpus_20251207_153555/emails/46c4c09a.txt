Good morning Loretta, Suzanne, and Kelsey,

I wanted to send a quick reminder about parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Staff parking is designated in the north lot only. Please do not park in student or visitor spaces.
• Arrive early enough to find a spot before the morning bell. The lot fills up quickly between 7:45-8:15 AM.
• Keep your vehicle parked in a single space and avoid blocking other cars.
• If you need to move your car during the day, please ensure you're not blocking the fire lane or handicap accessible areas.
• Report any parking concerns or safety issues to the main office.

Thank you for your cooperation and for helping us maintain an organized and safe parking environment for our students, staff, and visitors.
