Dear Samuel and Amy,

I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces
• Allow extra time in the morning for lot congestion during student drop-off (7:45-8:15 AM)
• Do not block fire lanes or handicapped accessible spaces
• Keep driving speeds low and watch for students crossing between vehicles
• Evening dismissal (3:15-3:45 PM) can also be congested—plan accordingly if you need to leave during this time

If you have any questions about parking assignments or need to request a different lot location, please contact the main office.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our parking areas safe and organized.
