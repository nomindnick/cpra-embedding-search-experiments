Good afternoon Nancy and Marc,

I wanted to send a quick reminder about our parking lot procedures as we head into the busy morning and afternoon dismissal times.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces in the upper lot
• Do not block fire lanes or loading zones at any time
• Keep vehicles locked and valuables out of sight
• Arrive early enough to allow time for safe parking
• Be mindful of student drop-off and pick-up zones during transition times

With the increased traffic during peak hours, please exercise caution when entering and exiting the parking area. If you notice any parking violations or safety concerns, please report them to the main office.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a safe and organized parking environment for our school community.
