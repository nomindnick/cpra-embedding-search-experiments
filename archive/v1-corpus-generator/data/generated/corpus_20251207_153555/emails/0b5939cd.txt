1. IEP meeting scheduling - We're going to shift to a more flexible window (30 days before/after the anniversary date) to reduce scheduling conflicts. This should help us get better attendance from families and staff.

3. Transition planning - We're expanding our transition services starting in 9th grade instead of 10th. I know this adds some work upfront, but feedback from our SPED coordinators suggests it gives students more time to explore options.
