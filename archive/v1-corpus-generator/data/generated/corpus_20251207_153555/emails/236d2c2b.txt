I wanted to send a quick reminder about our school parking lot procedures as we head into the busy season.

Please keep the following in mind:

• Park only in designated staff spaces in the main lot
• Do not block fire lanes or loading zones at any time
• Arrive early enough to find parking before the morning bell rings
• Be mindful of student drop-off zones during arrival and dismissal times
• Report any parking violations or safety concerns to the main office

We've had a few congestion issues during peak times, so your cooperation helps keep everyone safe. If you have any questions about parking assignments or need to request a different spot, please contact the main office.
