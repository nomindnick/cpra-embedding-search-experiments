I wanted to send a quick reminder about our Chromebook cart checkout process. I've noticed that some carts have been returned with devices that weren't properly shut down or charged, which affects availability for other classrooms.

Going forward, please ensure that:
- All devices are powered down before returning the cart
- Devices are placed in the charging dock
- The cart is locked and signed out in the log sheet
